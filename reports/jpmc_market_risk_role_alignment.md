# JPMC Market Risk Model Development Role Alignment

## Positioning

This project is positioned as a role-specific offline framework for fixed-income market-risk model development, not as a generic ML or fraud-risk project.

## Strong evidence

The strongest evidence is fixed-income pricing and sensitivities, corporate-credit spread factors, securitized-products proxy cash flows, VaR/ES/stressed VaR, rolling 250-day backtesting, assumption-impact analysis, and model-risk documentation.

## Relevant qualifications shown

Probability, time-series analysis, stress testing, backtesting, alternative assumption evaluation, Python implementation, model validation, documentation, and stakeholder-ready reporting are represented by executable artifacts.

## Remaining gap

The project does not replace professional fixed-income trading quant experience. Resume language should frame it as an offline, public-data-compatible portfolio framework under explicit synthetic/proxy boundaries.

## Suggested interview narrative

Lead with the end-to-end model-development lifecycle: data validation, risk-factor construction, valuation and sensitivities, VaR/ES/stress, backtesting, assumption impact, specialty reliability layer, and governance packet.
