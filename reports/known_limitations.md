# Known Limitations

## Data

Synthetic fallback or cached public-compatible data is not a substitute for audited market data, TRACE history, Bloomberg marks, or desk P&L.

## Portfolio

Synthetic positions approximate risk exposures but are not real bank positions.

## Securitized proxy

The tranche waterfall is a structural proxy, not production instrument valuation.

## Experience boundary

The project demonstrates technical readiness but does not represent professional fixed-income trading-desk employment.
