# Regulatory-Style Mapping Memo

## VaR and stressed VaR

The framework mirrors regulatory-style concepts around daily VaR, stressed VaR, and backtesting but is not a regulatory capital model.

## Backtesting

The backtesting design follows a 250-business-day, one-day 99% VaR exception-analysis pattern.

## Securitized products

Securitized exposure is represented by a synthetic proxy with explicit model limitations and compensating controls.

## Boundary

Regulatory-style and FRTB-inspired wording is used only for educational model-development artifacts; no compliance certification is claimed.
