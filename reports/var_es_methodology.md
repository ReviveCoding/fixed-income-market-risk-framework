# VaR and Expected Shortfall Methodology

## Methods

Historical simulation VaR, Monte Carlo VaR, Student-t VaR, Expected Shortfall, 10-day sqrt-time scaling, and stressed VaR are calculated.

## Headline validation-run results

Historical 99% VaR: 3,390,744.45; Historical 99% ES: 4,461,286.46; stressed VaR: 5,011,375.22.

## Boundary

These values are produced from an offline synthetic/proxy portfolio and are not represented as bank regulatory capital estimates.
